Who we are

About SK Bancal

The Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) is the youth council of Barangay Bancal, Carles, Iloilo. This council is led by Chair Hon. Nicole Anne D. Barbosa, term 2023 to 2026.

The SK runs sports leagues, scholarships, feeding programs, clean-ups and help for families, and posts its accomplishment reports on its Facebook profile.

This is a community information website made to support the SK. It is not an official government website. Posters, photos and the SK logo belong to the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Bancal. Design and code by Jolan Clark Sorillo.

The basics

How the SK works

Who is in the SK

Every young Filipino aged 15 to 30 who lives in the barangay belongs to the Katipunan ng Kabataan. They vote for the SK Chairperson and the council every few years. Anyone can join the activities.

What the SK does

It plans and runs programs for youth in Bancal: sports, education, health, the environment, livelihood and community service. Each year's plan is written into the ABYIP.

How to take part

Join a program, send an idea through the suggestion form, or message the SK on Facebook. Reports of finished projects are on the Reports page.
