Where the money comes from

The Sangguniang Kabataan receives a share of the barangay's general fund each year. It is spent through the Annual Barangay Youth Investment Program (ABYIP), which lists every planned project and its cost.

How to read the numbers

Planned is what the ABYIP set aside for a sector. Spent is what has actually been paid out. Remaining is the difference. The percent bar shows how much of the plan has been used.

Ask for a document

Anyone may ask to see the ABYIP, the SK budget or the liquidation reports. Send a message to the SK, or visit the barangay hall in Bancal, Carles.
