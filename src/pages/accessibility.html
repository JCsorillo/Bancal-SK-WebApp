Accessibility

This site is meant to work for everyone in Bancal, including people on slow phones, small screens, screen readers and keyboards.

What we have done

Every page has a skip link, headings in order, text descriptions for images, visible keyboard focus and large tap targets. Colour contrast was checked in both the light and dark themes. Animations are switched off for visitors whose device asks for reduced motion. The site works in English, Tagalog and Hiligaynon, and the emergency numbers are one tap away on every page.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press / or Ctrl+K to search the site. Press Esc to close search, menus and the poster viewer. Use the left and right arrow keys in the poster viewer.

Known gaps

Most report posters are images with a short description, not the full text of the poster. The Hiligaynon translation is a draft and is being reviewed. Some links lead to other websites that we do not control.

Tell us about a problem

If something here is hard to use or read, tell the SK through its contact page and say what device and browser you were using. We will try to fix it.

This is a community information website and is not an official government portal.
