What SK officials do

The council is small, so every member has a job.

The Chairperson

Leads the council, presides over meetings, and represents the youth of Bancal in the barangay council.

The Secretary

Keeps the records of meetings and resolutions so decisions can be checked later.

The Treasurer

Keeps the books for the SK fund: what comes in, what is paid out, and the receipts behind it.

The Kagawads

Each one looks after a committee, such as sports, education, health or the environment, and helps run its projects.
