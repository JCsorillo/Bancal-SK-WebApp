Who we are

This is a community information website made to support the Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) of Barangay Bancal, Carles, Iloilo. It is not an official government website. The site was designed and built by Jolan Clark Sorillo. You can reach the SK through its Facebook profile linked in the footer.

What this site saves on your device

Your language and light or dark mode choice, the posts you hearted, and whether you already counted as a visitor today. These stay in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Counters

When the shared counters are switched on, your browser sends the SK's counter service (a Google Apps Script) a heart click on a post, or one visit ping per day. No name, account or contact detail is sent. Like any web service, the host and Google may keep technical logs such as IP address under their own policies.

Suggestions

This site does not collect suggestions itself. The "Open the SK's suggestion form" button takes you to a Google Form run by the SK, which is covered by Google's own privacy policy and whatever the SK states on the form. Please do not write phone numbers, home addresses or other personal details of yourself or others in it.

Messages to the SK

The "Reach the SK" form only copies your message to your clipboard and opens Facebook. This site does not receive or store it. What you send on Facebook is handled under Facebook's own policies.

Other services this page uses

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Previews of government websites are pictures made by third-party screenshot services (Microlink and thum.io), so your browser contacts them when you view the Services section. Links to government sites, Facebook and the eGovPH app take you to services run by others, with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you may ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and you may object to how it is used. Contact the SK through its Facebook profile. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Young visitors

This site is for the youth of Bancal. If you are under 18, please do not give your name or any personal detail in any form, and ask a parent or guardian if you are unsure.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
