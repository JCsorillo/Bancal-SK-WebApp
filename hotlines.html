Stay calm

What to say when you call

  1. Say where you are.Barangay, street, and a landmark someone nearby would know.
  2. Say what happened.Fire, accident, medical emergency, or someone in danger.
  3. Say how many people are hurt.And whether anyone is not breathing or badly bleeding.
  4. Stay on the line.Give your name and number, and hang up only when they tell you to.
