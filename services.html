Save a trip

Before you go to the office

Check the official site first

Requirements, fees and schedules change. The agency's own website has the latest.

Bring a valid ID and copies

Many offices ask for one or two government IDs and a photocopy of each.

Skip the fixers

Pay only at the official cashier and ask for a receipt. Nobody outside the office can speed up your application.

Go early on weekdays

Queues are shortest in the morning, and many offices close for lunch.
