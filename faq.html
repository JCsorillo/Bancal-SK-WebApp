Frequently asked questions

Short answers about the SK and this website.

Is this the official website of the SK?

No. This is a community information website made to support the SK. Official announcements are posted on the SK's Facebook profile.

Where can I see the SK's latest news?

On the SK Bancal Facebook profile. This website keeps the accomplishment reports, projects and useful numbers in one place.

How do I suggest a program or project?

Use the SK's suggestion form (a Google Form). Your ideas guide the Annual Barangay Youth Investment Program (ABYIP) and the SK Budget. What gets included still depends on the youth's needs, SK priorities and available funds.

How do I contact the SK?

Message the SK through its Facebook profile. This website does not receive messages.

Where can I see what the SK has done?

The Reports page shows every accomplishment poster. The Projects page lists 49 projects, programs and activities for 2025 and 42 for 2023 to 2024.

What number do I call in an emergency?

Call 911 for any emergency. The Hotlines page lists local numbers from the Carles MDRRMO advisory (updated July 20, 2026) for medical, fire, police and sea rescue. Numbers can change.

Does this website collect my personal information?

No. It saves your language, light or dark mode and your hearts only on your own device. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Who made this website?

Jolan Clark Sorillo designed and built it for the SK. Posters, photos and the SK logo belong to the Sangguniang Kabataan of Barangay Bancal.

Something on the site is wrong or should be removed. What do I do?

Message the SK through its Facebook profile and say which page it is on, so it can be corrected or removed.

Got a question for the SK?

Questions about programs, scholarships or events? Write your message here and send it through the SK's Facebook profile.
